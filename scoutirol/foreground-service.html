ScouTirol — foreground service

Demonstration of FOREGROUND_SERVICE_LOCATION for the Google Play declaration. Package com.scoutirol, version 2.3.

Why the app needs the permission

ScouTirol plans and records hikes, bike rides and drives in South Tyrol. A recording has to survive a locked screen: people put the phone in a pocket and walk for hours. Without a foreground service Android stops delivering location updates, and the recorded track loses exactly the part that was walked in the meantime.

What the video shows

  1. The “Record” tab with a running recording. The user started it by tapping “Aufzeichnung starten” (“start recording”) — nothing starts on its own. The button now reads “Stoppen & speichern” (“stop & save”) and the timer counts up.
  2. The hint below the numbers states plainly: “Aufzeichnung läuft – du kannst das Handy einstecken und sperren. In der Leiste steht so lange eine Meldung.” (“Recording is running – you can pocket and lock the phone. A notification stays in the status bar while it does.”)
  3. The notification drawer shows the ongoing notification “ScouTirol zeichnet auf — Die Aufzeichnung läuft weiter, auch wenn der Bildschirm aus ist” (“ScouTirol is recording — the recording keeps running even when the display is off”).

The distance stays at 0.0 km because the recording was made indoors on a phone that did not move. A walk recorded with the same build on 28 September 2026 produced 110 points over 666 m with the screen locked and the phone in a pocket.

About the black areas in the video

Everything except the ScouTirol notification is masked in the drawer section, and the status bar is masked throughout. The original recording showed the owner's carrier, wallpaper and unrelated personal notifications. Nothing belonging to ScouTirol was removed or altered — the masking only covers private content of the device owner.

Scope

  • Recording starts only on an explicit tap and ends when the user ends it.
  • The positions stay on the device. There is no server and no account.
  • The app does not request ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION. The service is always started from the visible app.
  • The clip was recorded with a development build of the same version 2.3 on a Galaxy S23 (Android 16); the bundle under review is the release build of that version.
  • Privacy policy: dilidjent.github.io/scoutirol-privacy/

Kurz auf Deutsch

Die App zeichnet auf Wunsch eine Tour auf. Damit die Spur kein Loch bekommt, sobald der Bildschirm zugeht, läuft die Ortung in einem Vordergrunddienst vom Typ location weiter. Die Aufzeichnung beginnt ausschließlich auf Knopfdruck und endet, sobald der Nutzer sie beendet. Solange sie läuft, steht die Meldung „ScouTirol zeichnet auf“ in der Leiste. Die Positionen bleiben auf dem Gerät. Die schwarzen Flächen im Video decken Privates des Gerätebesitzers ab — Netzbetreiber, Hintergrundbild und fremde Meldungen —, nichts davon gehört zu ScouTirol.
