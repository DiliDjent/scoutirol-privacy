ScouTirol Winter — foreground service

Demonstration of FOREGROUND_SERVICE_LOCATION for the Google Play declaration. Package com.winterscout.winterscout_suedtirol.

Why the app needs the permission

ScouTirol Winter records ski tours, winter hikes and cross-country runs. A recording has to survive a locked screen: people put the phone in a pocket and walk for hours. Without a foreground service Android stops delivering location updates, and the recorded track gets a hole in the middle.

What the video shows

  1. The map screen. The user taps the red record button — nothing starts on its own.
  2. A sheet asks what is being recorded and states plainly that the recording keeps running while the display is off and that this costs battery.
  3. The user picks “Skitour”. The recording panel appears: elapsed time, distance, ascent, number of points. The recording was made indoors on a stationary phone, so distance stays at 0 m.
  4. The user leaves the app with the home button. The notification drawer shows the ongoing notification “Tour wird aufgezeichnet” (“recording a tour”), flagged ONGOING_EVENT | FOREGROUND_SERVICE.
  5. Back in the app the timer has kept counting — the service ran while the app was in the background.
  6. The user ends the recording. The service and its notification stop with it. Because the phone never moved, the app reports that there were too few points and that nothing was saved — the expected result for a recording made at a desk.

Scope

  • Recording starts only on an explicit tap and ends when the user ends it.
  • The positions stay on the device. There is no server and no account.
  • The app does not request ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION.
  • Privacy policy: dilidjent.github.io/scoutirol-privacy/winterscout/

Kurz auf Deutsch

Die App zeichnet auf Wunsch eine Tour auf. Damit die Spur kein Loch bekommt, sobald der Bildschirm zugeht, läuft die Ortung in einem Vordergrunddienst vom Typ location weiter. Die Aufzeichnung beginnt ausschließlich auf Knopfdruck und endet, sobald der Nutzer sie beendet. Solange sie läuft, steht die Meldung „Tour wird aufgezeichnet“ in der Leiste, sofern Benachrichtigungen erlaubt sind. Die Positionen bleiben auf dem Gerät.
