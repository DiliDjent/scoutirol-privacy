Privacy Policy – WinterScout Alpi

Last updated: 18 September 2026

DeutschItalianoEnglish

In short

The app has no user accounts on a server, no advertising and no analytics services. What you enter stays on your device. When loading maps, weather and avalanche danger, the respective service sees your IP address – like any website.

Controller

Diego Frisanco, Italy. Reachable at the e-mail address at the bottom of this page. No data protection officer is named, because the app transmits no data to us.

Location

Your location is used only on the device: for the distance in lists, the map centre, the nearest weather zone and the emergency page. It is not transmitted to us. A recording (tour) is stored by the app locally only; you can delete it at any time. While a recording is running, the app keeps locating you even with the screen locked – Android shows a permanent notification for this, which disappears once you stop the recording. Without location permission the app keeps working, just without distances.

What third parties see

Map tiles come from OpenFreeMap, the weather from MET Norway, avalanche bulletins from avalanche.report and the EAWS archives. These services receive the request (map section or coordinates of a weather zone) and your IP address. Their own privacy policies apply to them. If you tap a ski area website or a source link on the About page, your browser opens an external site; from there its rules apply. The emergency call button only opens the dialler; the app does not place calls itself and sends no message without your action. The text on the emergency page contains a map link with your coordinates – it is only sent if you send it yourself.

What is stored on the device

Map layer settings, favourites, downloaded tiles, your own spots, imported and recorded tours, and the cache for weather, avalanche danger and tour tracks. You can delete all of it in the app: tiles, cache and map layers under Profile → Offline, favourites with the same heart you added them with, your own spots and individual tours on their detail page. Uninstalling removes everything.

Account and community

This version of the app has no sign-in: there is no server that could hold an account, and therefore no sign-in screen either. Ratings and favourites stay on the device. Should a server be connected in the future, this policy will be updated beforehand.

Purchase

The app is bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment; we receive no payment details, no name and no address. There are no in-app purchases. Google’s privacy policy additionally applies to the purchase.

Permissions

Location (optional): distances, map centre, emergency page, recording. Foreground service and keeping the device awake: only while a recording is running, so the track does not break off with the screen locked. Notification: shows that a recording is running – without it, it would keep running invisibly. Internet: maps, weather, avalanche danger. No camera permission and no phone permission: the app cannot dial by itself.

Your rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and data portability, as well as the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (in Italy: Garante per la protezione dei dati personali). That is quickly done here: we hold no data about you. Everything the app stores is on your device and can be deleted there.

Changes

If what the app processes changes, this policy is changed beforehand; the date above says when it last happened. The version shown in the app and available at the address linked in the Play Store always applies.
